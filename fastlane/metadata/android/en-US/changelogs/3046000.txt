New: strains. Keep a list of the strains you use, with optional THC, CBD and cost per gram. Pick one when you log a use: its cost fills in, the use card shows it, and the filter finds uses by strain. Manage them in Settings, Strains. Backups carry each use's strain. Importing a backup whose notes have a line break works now, and Petals tells you how many uses an import brought in, or that the file couldn't be imported.
